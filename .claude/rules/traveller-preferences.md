# Traveller preferences — apply to every search, shortlist and booking

Stated by the operator (2026-10-26). Rank options by these, not by price alone.

## Connections: relaxed over fast

The operator **prefers relaxed transfers and will trade a longer trip for peace of mind.**

- **Protected connections first.** One ticket (or a carrier's own through-fare, e.g. AirAsia
  "Fly-Thru") beats separate tickets or self-transfer at the same or a slightly higher price.
  Flag every self-transfer or unlabelled connection explicitly.
- **Longer layovers rank higher.** Treat a connection under **~2 h** as tight, and prefer
  **~3 h or more**, especially for international transfers, terminal changes or a re-check of
  bags. (These thresholds are a working default, not the operator's words; adjust if told.)
  With `fli`, pass `min_layover` instead of filtering afterwards.
- **A shorter itinerary is not a tiebreaker** when it costs connection slack. Say what the
  extra time buys ("3 h 40 m at <hub>, protected") next to the price.

## Example

A 3 h 40 m protected (Fly-Thru) connection is picked over a 2 h unlabelled one at the same
price, and the shortlist says so next to the fare.
